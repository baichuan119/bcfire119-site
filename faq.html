FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical compliance, manufacturing timelines, and custom configuration support before you initiate your B2B inquiry.

What standards do your fire sprinklers conform to?

Our core fire sprinkler ranges are manufactured and hydrostatically evaluated according to standard international requirements. For specific target projects, we supply detailed technical submittals, testing curves, and compliance sheets corresponding to major certifications (UL, FM, CE) for exact K-factor and bulb sizes.

Can you configure custom connection threads?

Yes. We support all common global thread standards, including BSPT, NPT, and specific regional requirements such as Russian GOST couplers or British Standard (BS 21) threads. Please state your exact requirement in your RFQ message.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our minimum order quantity depends on the product category. For fire sprinklers, typical MOQs start at 1,000 to 2,000 pieces due to automated casting setup costs. For larger system components like wet alarm valves, deluge systems, and pillar hydrants, we support low MOQs (from 5 to 50 sets) to assist project-specific package sourcing.

Do you support OEM/ODM branding and custom packaging?

Yes. Baichuan Fire has more than 15 years of OEM/ODM export experience. We support custom nameplate engraving, product body branding, customized inner box styling, and seaworthy plywood crates. Share your packaging specifications with our team during the inquiry.

What is the typical production lead time?

Standard components from our active production line typically ship in 15 to 25 days. Large project packages or custom sand-castings requiring structural mould adjustments can take 30 to 45 days. We will provide an estimated delivery schedule upon reviewing your bill of materials.
