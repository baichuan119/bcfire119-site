COMPANY PROFILE

Fluid Control Solutions For Global Projects.

Based in Quanzhou, Fujian — the core cluster of China's plumbing and valve industry — Quanzhou Baichuan Firefighting Equipment Co., Ltd. manufactures and exports industrial valves, hydraulic control products, hydrant components, and fire protection equipment for international B2B projects. Manufacturing runs on our own production lines in Quanzhou, with selected items produced by qualified manufacturing partners; valve bodies are CNC-machined and every unit is shell pressure and air-leakage tested before shipment, under an ISO 9001 quality management system.

We build every product page around the specifications buyers actually specify from — pressure class, connection standard, body and trim material, and application — with inquiry-based quotations and engineering support for water supply, industrial fluid control, and firefighting systems.

PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

Our Manufacturing Process

Two lines, one finishing discipline: hose jackets are woven and lined in-house, valve and hydrant bodies are CNC-machined, and every unit is pressure tested before coating and packing.

01

Precision Weaving

High-strength polyester weaving for durable fire hose jackets. (Fire hose)

02

Lining Bonding

Advanced vulcanization process for bubble-tight rubber lining. (Fire hose)

03

CNC Machining

Precision machining for ductile iron valve bodies and components. (Valves & hydrants)

04

Safety Testing

100% shell pressure and air leakage testing for every unit. (All lines)

05

Epoxy Coating

Non-toxic epoxy resin coating for superior corrosion resistance. (Valves & hydrants)
