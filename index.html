MANUFACTURING DISCIPLINE

Our 5-Step Precision Manufacturing Process

From raw iron castings to the final epoxy powder coating, every stage of our production is optimized for reliability and zero-tolerance defects.

01

Precision CNC Machining

Ductile iron valve bodies and brass sprinkler frames are machined on advanced computer-controlled lathes to maintain tight tolerance limits.

02

100% Safety Testing

Every individual unit undergoes rigorous hydrostatic shell pressure and seat air-leakage tests to guarantee bubble-tight sealing.

03

Advanced Lining & Bonding

Our layflat hoses undergo specialized high-temperature vulcanization to securely bond durable rubber linings to polyester jackets.

04

Electrostatic Epoxy Coating

All cast iron bodies are coated with high-grade, non-toxic red epoxy powder to provide superior rust and corrosion resistance.

05

Finishing & Packing

Final components are assembled with position indicators, securely labeled, and packed in heavy-duty sea-freight wooden crates.
