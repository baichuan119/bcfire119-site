Fire Hose Reels & Cabinets

Systematic, rapid-response firefighting enclosures and components engineered for architectural safety, durability, and smooth fluid control.

Quick First-Response Solutions

In the event of a localized incident, response speed is paramount. Baichuan Fire supplies highly reliable wall-mounted manual & automatic fire hose reels, heavy-duty flexible PVC/rubber pressure hoses, adjustable spray-fog-straight nozzles, and integrated steel protective enclosures. Our reels feature robust brass swivel joint fittings and center-mounted pressure gauges for real-time safety evaluations.

Available Options

  • Wall-Mounted Hose Reels: 3/4 inch and 1 inch manual/automatic swivel-type hose reels with lengths up to 30 meters.
  • PVC & Rubber Hoses: Flexible, high-elasticity hoses with anti-corrosion properties and reliable working pressure thresholds.
  • Adjustable Nozzles: High-performance brass or chrome-finished nozzles capable of switching from fine mist fog to long-distance straight stream.
  • Fire Cabinets & Enclosures: Heavy-gauge galvanized steel or stainless steel single and double-compartment boxes with transparent glass windows and custom locking mechanisms.
