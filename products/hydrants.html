Fire Hydrants & Landing Valves

Heavy-duty municipal and commercial water outlet devices engineered for seamless coupling and robust pressure control under critical conditions.

Reliable Water Access Points

Baichuan Fire manufactures robust indoor landing valves and outdoor ground pillar hydrants constructed from high-quality ductile cast iron and forged brass. Our hydrant components are tested for 1.6 MPa working pressure and feature custom regional standard couplings to match local fire brigade thread requirements, ensuring rapid access in emergency scenarios.

Available Options

  • Indoor Landing Valves: DN50/DN65 angular or straight valves with built-in pressure reducing options for high-rise buildings.
  • Pillar Hydrants (BS750): High-visibility red outdoor ground pillars with flanged underground pipe connectors.
  • Underground Hydrants: Space-saving subsurface hydrants designed to fit under cast-iron protective covers.
  • Breeching Inlets / Siamese Connections: Flanged ductile iron 2-way and 4-way connections allowing fire trucks to feed water into internal wet pipe sprinkler systems.
