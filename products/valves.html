PRODUCT CATELOGUE / 03

System Valves & Control Assemblies

Heavy-duty, hydrostatically tested isolation and control valves designed to maintain pressure integrity and control water flow in sprinkler and hydrant networks.

Rigorous Pressure Management

Valves are the critical nodes of any fire network. Baichuan Fire manufactures robust gate valves, butterfly valves, swing check valves, automatic air vents, wet alarm check valves and diaphragm deluge alarm valve systems. Every single valve is subjected to high-pressure seat leak testing and shell testing up to 2.4 MPa, exceeding rated working pressures to ensure dependable operation in crisis scenarios.

Available Options

  • Gate Valves (OS&Y / NRS): Resilient wedge gate valves in flanged or grooved formats with visual open/close stems.
  • Butterfly Valves: Compact, high-flow butterfly valves equipped with tamper-proof supervisory switches to communicate valve state to central control panels.
  • Swing Check Valves: Reliable, non-return check valves that prevent water backflow in high-pressure networks.
  • Automatic Air Release Valves: High-pressure vents designed to discharge air from wet pipe sprinkler systems automatically.
