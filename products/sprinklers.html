Fire Sprinklers & Discharge Systems

Providing high-reliability, thermally sensitive automatic sprinkler heads engineered for prompt response and uniform water coverage.

Engineered for Thermal Precision

Baichuan Fire's automatic sprinklers utilize premium, responsive glass bulbs calibrated to accurate burst temperatures (57°C, 68°C, 79°C, 93°C). Each sprinkler frame is machined from durable copper alloy castings with corrosion-resistant finishes (chrome or natural brass) to fit seamlessly into modern industrial, commercial, and retail protection networks.

Available Options

  • Pendent Sprinklers (ZSTX): Classic downward discharge layout for suspended ceiling configurations.
  • Upright Sprinklers (ZSTZ): Upward discharge for exposed overhead structural piping.
  • Sidewall Sprinklers (ZSTBS): Horizontal discharge for corridor, hallway and wall-mounted setups.
  • Concealed Sprinklers: Flat, aesthetic finish covers for modern luxury commercial architecture.
  • ESFR (Early Suppression Fast Response): K16.8 / K25.2 heavy-duty high-flow discharge for demanding high-rack warehouse safety.
