WHO WE ARE

Our Manufacturing Foundations

Established in 2014 in Quanzhou, Fujian—China’s premier manufacturing hub for valves and plumbing hardware—Quanzhou Baichuan Firefighting Equipment Co., Ltd. has developed into a reliable, integrated manufacturer. We focus on structural fire safety components, supplying overseas project contractors, system integrators, and distributors.

Our core manufacturing facilities cover more than 5,000 square meters, equipped with high-precision CNC machining centers, automated valve drilling systems, and standard hydrotesting loops. Being a manufacturer, we control every step from sand casting evaluation and dimensional machining to hydraulic seat leak testing and powder coating thickness.

Strict Technical Verification

  • Casting integrity: Evaluating ductile iron and forged brass body density to prevent pressure-induced porosity.
  • Mechanical tolerances: Precise thread geometry (NPT, BSPT, BS 21) for leak-free system integration on site.
  • 100% Leak test: Every single alarm check valve, deluge valve, and landing valve undergoes rigorous hydrostatic seat and shell testing.
